**What happened:** According to its release notes, OpenAI started rolling out **ChatGPT Space** to **Plus** users aged 18 and older on 20 November 2026, globally. Until now Space was limited to Pro, Business and Enterprise plans (we covered it [after DevDay in September](/en/ai/chatgpt-space-pages-schuzky)). On Plus it replaces Library and becomes the home for pages, presentations, images and uploaded files. You can create interactive pages and presentations in it, invite other people with Space to work on them with you, and set the content to update from connected apps. Creating and editing works on the web, in the desktop app and on iOS and Android. The rollout is gradual, so Space may not appear in your account right away.

**What it means for you:** Most individuals and freelancers are on Plus, so this is the moment the feature reaches people without a company account. In practice three things change:

- **The output doesn't vanish in a chat.** Instead of an answer you can't find a week later, you get a page you come back to. Typical uses are a project overview, client briefing material or a personal weekly plan.
- **The page maintains itself.** OpenAI gives the examples of a to-do list that fills itself in and a project plan that reflects decisions made in Slack. A prompt could be: "Create a page with the status of the Novák contract. Every morning add new emails from the client and open tasks."
- **Edits through a comment.** You tag ChatGPT in a comment on the page and say what to change. No need to copy text back into a conversation.

Keep in mind that Space is still more of a tool for your own work and for people with a paid account than a replacement for a shared document you can send to anyone.

**How to get started:**

1. Open ChatGPT and look for **Space** in the sidebar. It sits where Library used to be. If it isn't there, the rollout hasn't reached you yet.
2. Check that your existing files and images from Library have carried over.
3. Build your first page from something you already do by hand. A weekly overview works well: "Make a page with my priorities for this week, a checklist and room for notes."
4. Only then add a connected app (email, calendar, Slack) and let the page update automatically. Start with one source so you can see what changes on the page and why.
5. When you want a change, tag ChatGPT in a comment right next to the part it concerns.

**What to watch out for:**

- **Sharing has a bottleneck.** A shared page or presentation can only be opened by someone you gave permission to and who is signed in to a Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise account with Space. People on the free plan can't open it and public links aren't available. For a client without a paid account, send an export, not a link.
- **Automatic updates are not a review.** A page that fills itself from email or Slack can pick up a mistake or pull a sentence out of context. The rule "AI proposes, you approve" applies: before you promise a client anything based on such a page, check the figure at the source.
- **Dots are not included.** The always-on agents OpenAI calls dots are not switched on automatically with this expansion to Plus. If you read about them in other coverage, they are most likely not in your account.
- **Scope depends on the plan.** Space is part of a paid plan. Limits and differences between plans are listed on the [plans overview](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) page.
