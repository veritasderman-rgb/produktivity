**What happened:** On 10 November 2026 Anthropic announced **Claude Meetings**, and two days later the feature appeared in the release notes. Claude joins a **Google Meet call as a visible participant**: it takes notes, answers questions and, when asked, creates a document, whiteboard or slide deck during the meeting. The feature is in **beta for Team and Enterprise plans**, in Claude on the web and in the desktop app. Video call tools other than Google Meet are not supported yet. Alongside it, Anthropic introduced shared Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets, which according to press coverage are in beta for paid plans.

**What it means for you:** There are plenty of meeting notetakers on the market. The difference here is that Claude doesn't wait for the call to end. Work you used to do "after the meeting" gets done during it:

- **Notes and action items without a notetaker** — after the call, the meeting page holds a summary, a list of action items and the transcript. Invited colleagues from your organization receive a recap email with a link.
- **Answers from company material** — say its name or type in the chat, for example "Claude, what deadline did we promise the client last time?". The answer appears in the call chat.
- **A draft ready before the call ends** — "Claude, turn what we just discussed into a draft timeline" and the document is attached to the meeting page, where the team can keep editing it.
- **Looking things up later** — in regular chat you can ask what was agreed in which meeting.

For a small team this means nobody has to spend half the meeting typing and the other half catching up on what was said. The quality of the notes still depends on how well Claude understood the conversation. The help article doesn't list supported languages, so if you don't meet in English, try it on an internal meeting first.

**How to get started:**

1. Check that you are on a Team or Enterprise plan. On Team the feature is on by default, on Enterprise an owner has to enable it in the organization settings (Organization settings → Products → Claude Meetings).
2. Open **Meetings** in Claude and connect Google Calendar. The connector must be enabled for the whole organization.
3. Pick a meeting from your calendar and click **Add Claude**.
4. At the start of the call, tell participants that Claude is taking notes. It also posts a notice in the chat with a link to the meeting page.
5. Turn on auto-join for all meetings (Settings → Capabilities → Meetings) only after you have got a feel for the feature.

**What to watch out for:**

- **Participant consent.** Claude saves a transcript, a summary and descriptions of the shared screen. According to the help article it does not save audio. Before adding it, make sure everyone is fine with being transcribed. For meetings with employees and clients, keep GDPR in mind too.
- **External guests.** On Team plans, joining calls with people outside the company is allowed by default, on Enterprise an owner has to approve it. Guests don't get the meeting page or the recap, but their words are in the transcript. Consider whether you want that in sales negotiations.
- **AI proposes, you approve.** When Claude is asked to use a connected tool during the call, such as sending a Slack message, it asks that person for permission. Read the notes and action items before sharing them. A wrongly assigned task is harder to fix than a typo.
- **Limits and beta status.** Actions through connected tools count toward your plan's usage. See the [paid plans](https://claude.com/pricing) page for details. The feature is not available to organizations with zero data retention or customer-managed encryption keys.
