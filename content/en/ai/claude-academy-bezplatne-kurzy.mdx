**What happened:** On August 20, 2026, Anthropic launched **Claude Academy** – a free learning platform at academy.claude.com, built on how the company trains its own employees. Courses are free, require no credit card, and award a badge on completion. The lineup ranges from **Claude 101** for complete beginners, through practical guides on Projects, Research mode, Skills, and connectors, to an **AI Fluency** course that teaches the **4D framework** – Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence – how to hand a task to AI sensibly, instead of just "throw a prompt at it and hope."

**What it means for you:** Instead of everyone figuring out how AI works by trial and error, you get a structured path tested on thousands of employees at one of the leading AI companies:

- **A fast start without frustration** – the Claude 101 course walks through the basics of prompting and artifacts, so your first week with Claude doesn't feel like a gamble.
- **Concrete work scenarios** – the academy also has courses built around everyday tasks: planning your week, analyzing a proposal, turning research into a presentation.
- **A 4D framework for delegating work** – Delegation (what to hand off to AI), Description (how to phrase the request), Discernment (how to judge the result), and Diligence (when to double-check it) is a useful approach beyond Claude, with any AI assistant.

For teams still figuring out how to roll out AI, this is a faster path than building internal training from scratch – instead of putting together your own "how we use AI here" presentation, you can send colleagues a link to a ready-made, tested course and spend your time discussing cases specific to your own work.

**How to get started:**

1. Go to **academy.claude.com** (or in the Claude app, click your profile and choose "Learn more").
2. Pick a starting point based on your level: Claude 101 to begin, AI Fluency for the 4D framework, or a course built around a specific task (planning, research, presentations).
3. Courses are self-paced – work through them at your own speed, and the badge you earn on completion also works as material for training colleagues internally.

**Watch out for:** The academy is currently aimed mainly at English-speaking users, and some of the more technical courses (API, Claude Code) target developers rather than everyday users – for purely work-related use, stick to the non-developer section and the AI Fluency courses. The badge is a motivational marker, not a formal certification with guaranteed value to an employer, so present it as evidence you took the time to learn the topic, not as a credential.
