**What happened:** Todoist has launched an official MCP server (Model Context Protocol) — an open standard through which AI assistants access your data securely. Once connected, ChatGPT can **read your tasks and projects, create new ones and edit existing ones** (rename, reschedule, move) inside your Todoist. It works on the Beginner, Pro and Business plans.

**What it means for you:** Things that used to mean ten minutes of clicking now take one conversation:

- **Inbox triage** — “go through my inbox and suggest which items are quick wins for today”
- **Weekly review** — “what didn't I finish this week? reschedule it sensibly into next week”
- **Brain dump → plan** — you dictate a messy stream of thoughts and the AI turns it into clean tasks with sections and due dates

This is exactly the shift we write about in the guide: AI is no longer just a typewriter — it's starting to operate your tools.

**How to try it today:**

1. In ChatGPT, enable **Developer mode (beta)**: Settings → Plugins (in newer versions of ChatGPT the section is called **Apps**, or Connectors) → Advanced Settings.
2. In the same section, add a custom plugin/app with the URL `https://ai.todoist.net/mcp`.
3. Sign in to Todoist and approve the permissions.

**Careful:** the connection can write, too — keep changes in approval mode in ChatGPT so the AI doesn't reschedule your life without asking. You can disconnect at any time from either side.
