The meeting's starting, the projector's connected, and there's nothing on the screen — while everyone waits for you to “get it working.” A classic that repeats in every conference room and every client presentation, and yet it has a one-keypress fix that hardly anyone knows about.

## A typical scenario

You arrive at a meeting, plug the cable into the projector or connect via wireless display, but the screen still shows only your laptop's desktop, or nothing at all. You start clicking through Settings, hunting for the Display section, waiting for the system to figure out what to do with the second display — while coworkers or a client wait and the mood gets tenser.

With the <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> shortcut, you avoid all of that. Press two keys, a small menu with four modes appears on screen, and you pick the one you need — the whole thing takes a few seconds and there's no settings menu to wade through.

## How to do it

1. Press <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd>. A side panel with display modes appears on screen.
2. Choose based on the situation: **PC screen only** (the projector stays black), **Duplicate** (the same image on both displays — ideal for presentations), **Extend** (the second monitor works as extra desktop space), or **Second screen only** (the laptop screen goes dark, the image goes only to the projector).
3. Use the arrow keys or the mouse to select the option you want, then confirm with Enter or a click.
4. If the image still doesn't show up on the projector after picking a mode, check the physical cable connection or the wireless display pairing — the shortcut fixes the software side, not the hardware.

## The best tools

- **The built-in Win + P shortcut** — works on any Windows computer with nothing to install, the fastest way to switch display modes.
- **Settings → System → Display** — the same option, just a longer route through the menu, useful when you want to fine-tune the display further (resolution, orientation).
- **Graphics card drivers (Intel, NVIDIA, AMD)** — their own control panels offer more advanced multi-monitor settings, including scaling and color profiles.
- **Wireless display (Miracast)** — if the projector supports a wireless connection, you skip hunting for a cable entirely; pairing is under **Connect** in the Action Center.

## What you get out of it

- **Time:** no more awkward pause at the start of a meeting while everyone waits for you to “get it working” — a two-keypress fix instead of wandering through settings.
- **Composure:** the confidence that in a high-pressure moment you know exactly what to do, even in front of an audience.
- **A professional impression:** a smooth transition into a presentation looks far better than visible fumbling with cables and menus.
- **Portability:** the shortcut works the same way on every work or personal computer, no need to remember a different procedure for a different conference room.

## Pro tip

For working at home with an external monitor, the best setup is to permanently choose **Extend** — and move windows between screens with <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>←</kbd>/<kbd>→</kbd>, without ever needing to go back to the Win + P menu again.
