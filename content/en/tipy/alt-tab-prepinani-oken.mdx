Moving your hand from keyboard to mouse and back — that's a second or two, every single time. Switch windows a hundred times a day and it adds up to minutes you never even notice losing. Almost everyone knows <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd>, but most people only use its simplest form — one quick tap, jump to the last window, and nothing more.

## A typical scenario

Petr, an analyst, has Excel, email, a browser with five tabs, and a notes document all open. To switch to a specific window, he reaches for the mouse, moves to the taskbar, hunts for a moment among similar-looking icons, and clicks. Ten times an hour, forty times a day — and each time his hand leaves the keyboard, so he has to find his place again to keep typing.

With <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd>, his hand never leaves the keyboard at all. A quick tap jumps to the last window; holding it down shows previews of every open window, and he picks with the arrow keys. A switch that used to take two or three seconds with a detour to the mouse now takes a fraction of a second, with no interruption to his typing.

## How to do it

1. Tap <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> briefly — you jump to the **previous** window. Ideal when you're constantly switching between two apps (say, a document and a browser).
2. Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> and keep tapping <kbd>Tab</kbd> — previews of all open windows appear and you cycle through them. Releasing <kbd>Alt</kbd> confirms whichever one you land on.
3. If you overshoot, there's no need to go all the way around again — hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> as well and cycle the other direction: <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd>.
4. <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> opens Task View with all windows **and virtual desktops** at once — useful when you work across multiple desktops and are hunting for a window across all of them.
5. In Task View from <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd>, you can also drag windows between virtual desktops with the mouse, not just switch between them.

## The best tools

- **Built-in Alt+Tab and Win+Tab in Windows** — nothing to install, works on every Windows computer.
- **PowerToys — Alt+Tab enhancements** — a free add-on from Microsoft that lets you filter which windows show up or change how the previews look.
- **PowerToys FancyZones** — if you're switching windows a lot simply because you have too many open on screen, this helps by laying them out into fixed zones instead.
- **On Mac:** the equivalent role is played by <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> for switching apps and <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>˜</kbd> for switching between windows of the same app.

## What you get out of it

- **Time:** a few seconds per switch, across dozens of switches a day, adds up to roughly 3 minutes that would otherwise go into the trip to the mouse and back.
- **Smoother work:** your hands never leave the keyboard, so your typing flow and focus don't get interrupted.
- **Fewer mistakes:** cycling through previews is more accurate than clicking tiny taskbar icons, where similar apps are easy to mix up.

## Pro tip

If you work across multiple virtual desktops, combine <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> for a quick overview across desktops with classic <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> for switching within one desktop — together they cover the vast majority of situations where you'd otherwise reach for the mouse. Try tracking for a week how often you actually reach for the mouse just to switch windows — most people are surprised how high the number is, and how quickly it drops once the shortcut becomes a habit.
