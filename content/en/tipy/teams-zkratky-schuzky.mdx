“You're muted!” is the most common sentence uttered in any meeting — and also the most needless waste of time, since the fix is one keystroke, not fumbling with the cursor to find the right icon at the bottom of the screen. Anyone who runs meetings often will especially appreciate keyboard shortcuts: they save seconds that, across dozens of meetings a week, add up to real minutes.

## A typical scenario

You're in a meeting with ten people, sharing a presentation on your second monitor, and the Teams control panel is hidden behind another window. Someone unexpectedly asks you something — and instead of answering right away, you spend three seconds hunting with the mouse for where to click to unmute. In the meantime, the host fixes it for you, or the question gets repeated and you come across as not paying attention.

With shortcuts, you press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> and start talking, whether the Teams window is visible or buried under your presentation — the shortcuts work globally, not just in the active meeting window.

## How to do it

1. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> — turns your microphone on or off. It works even when the Teams window isn't in the foreground, which is exactly when you need it, while sharing your screen with something else.
2. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>O</kbd> — turns your camera on or off, the same way as the microphone shortcut.
3. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> — raises your hand, the digital equivalent of a raised hand in the room; the host sees you in the queue and can call on you without you having to talk over anyone else.
4. Remember the shortcuts by the pattern: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> plus the first letter of the action (M for Microphone, O for camera On/Off, K for the raised hand — think “knock”) — helpful once you have several shortcuts to keep straight for good.
5. If you're not sure of the exact shortcut mid-meeting, hover the cursor over the corresponding icon in the control panel — Teams shows a tooltip with the keyboard shortcut right there.

## The best tools

- **Teams' built-in keyboard shortcuts** — work instantly, no setup required, even with the meeting window in the background.
- **An external meeting controller** (some wireless headsets and webcams have a physical mute button) — good for people who prefer a physical control over the keyboard.
- **A programmable key or macro button** (on gaming keyboards or stream decks) mapped to the mute shortcut — for anyone with a truly packed daily meeting schedule.
- **Meeting settings in Teams** (auto-mute on join) — a useful complement to the shortcuts for large meetings where people commonly join gradually.

## What you get out of it

- **Time:** even a few seconds saved on every mic toggle adds up to whole minutes a day for anyone with a calendar full of meetings.
- **A more professional impression:** reacting instantly without fumbling for a button looks confident, especially when you're the one running the meeting.
- **Fewer awkward pauses:** “hold on, let me unmute” disappears from the vocabulary of the meetings you're in.
- **Reliability during screen sharing:** the shortcuts work globally, so you're not dependent on whether the Teams window happens to be visible.

## Pro tip

Holding <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Space</kbd> works like a walkie-talkie: you're unmuted while you hold the keys down, and automatically muted again the moment you let go. Handy for short interjections in the middle of someone else's presentation, when you don't want to bother toggling the mic on and off manually.
