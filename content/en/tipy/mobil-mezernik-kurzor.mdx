The most annoying thing about typing on a phone has an elegant fix. How many times have you tried to land a finger exactly between two letters to fix a typo, only to accidentally delete the whole word or select an entirely different chunk of text instead? Almost nobody knows this small gesture, even though it's been built into both major mobile systems for years.

## A typical scenario

You're writing a longer message and notice a typo in the middle of the third sentence. The usual approach: try to land your finger precisely right after the wrong letter, the phone picks a different spot instead, you delete too much, retype, and the result is worse than the original typo. Frustration builds with every attempt, especially on a smaller screen or with bigger fingers. What should've taken two seconds stretches into half a minute of fixing fixes — and this exact small annoyance repeats itself many times a day for anyone who writes longer text on a phone.

Instead of hunting for one exact spot with a finger, just hold down the space bar. The keyboard briefly turns gray and becomes a surface where dragging your finger moves the cursor exactly where you need it — as smoothly as moving a cursor with a mouse on a computer. You land it right the first time, no fixing fixes required.

## How to do it

1. While typing, **hold down the space bar** (on some systems, a light press-and-drag works too, without a classic long hold — try both).
2. The keyboard turns gray and the text beneath it becomes a sensitive surface — dragging your finger left, right, up, or down moves the cursor in the text to match exactly.
3. Once the cursor is in the right spot, lift your finger — the keyboard returns to normal and you continue typing or deleting from there.
4. The gesture works on both iPhone and Android with the Gboard keyboard, nearly identically on both platforms.
5. For longer jumps (say, to the start or end of a paragraph), the gesture is faster than repeated arrow-key taps or finger-hunting — it's worth using even for moves that aren't just a single word away.
6. Try the gesture for deleting whole words or sentences too — once the cursor lands at the start of the problem passage, combine the trackpad move with regular deleting instead of deleting character by character.

Your hands take a bit of time to adjust to the space bar suddenly doing something other than adding a space — but after a few days of use, the gesture becomes automatic and you'll reach for it without thinking.

## The best tools

- **The iOS keyboard's built-in gesture** — works on every iPhone with nothing extra to install.
- **Gboard (Android)** — the same feature, plus the option to adjust gesture sensitivity in the keyboard settings.
- **SwiftKey** — an alternative keyboard from Microsoft with a similar cursor-movement gesture, good if you prefer a different layout or predictive typing.
- **The iOS keyboard's trackpad via 3D Touch/Haptic Touch** — on older iPhones that support pressure sensitivity, a similar trick works through a firmer press on the space bar.

## What you get out of it

- **Time:** a few minutes a day for people who type a lot on their phone and often fix typos.
- **Precision:** the cursor lands exactly where you want it, the first time — no more fixing fixes.
- **Calm:** less frustration from a small keyboard and imprecise finger taps on text.
- **Writing speed:** fewer breaks in your train of thought while writing longer messages or notes.
