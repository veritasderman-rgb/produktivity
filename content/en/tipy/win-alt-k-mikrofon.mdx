You're sharing notes at a meeting, looking something up in a second window — and right then you need to say something quickly. Or the opposite: someone's knocking at the door and you need to mute yourself instantly. But the meeting window is buried somewhere under five others and you're frantically hunting for it through Alt+Tab. Windows 11 has a system-wide shortcut for exactly this: Win+Alt+K toggles microphone muting from anywhere, no matter which app you're currently in.

## How to do it

1. During a call, press <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> — the microphone mutes, and a brief confirmation with the mic's status appears on screen.
2. Press the same shortcut again to unmute — ideal for a quick “just one comment” and back to silence.
3. The shortcut is part of Windows 11 and works most reliably with apps that support system-level call controls — Microsoft Teams above all. With other apps, test it once before an important meeting.
4. When an app is using the microphone, Windows 11 shows a microphone icon on the taskbar near the clock — clicking it lets you mute the call with the mouse too, and it also serves as a check: a crossed-out icon means you really can't be heard.

## A typical scenario

An online class: the teacher is sharing a presentation and, in the meantime, looking up an answer to a student's question in the browser. In the background, her dog starts barking. Instead of hunting for the Teams window among the presentation, the browser, and her notes, she presses Win+Alt+K — muted within a second. Once the dog decides it's successfully defended the mail, she uses the same shortcut to return to the lesson.

A manager's version: a long meeting with twelve people, where you speak once every twenty minutes. You stay muted, work in other windows, and when a question comes your way, you answer instantly — Win+Alt+K, a sentence, Win+Alt+K — instead of an awkward pause of “…hang on, looking for the button.”

## What you get out of it

- **Reacting without hunting:** muting and unmuting stops depending on where the meeting window happens to be.
- **Fewer embarrassing moments:** muting fast for a sudden noise — a doorbell, kids, a dog — is a matter of one shortcut, not five seconds of panic.
- **Certainty:** the microphone icon on the taskbar tells you plainly, any time, whether you can be heard.
- **One reflex for every meeting:** the shortcut is system-wide, so you learn it once — and don't need to remember where the mute button lives in each individual app.

## Pro tip

If you meet in Teams, it's also worth knowing the [shortcuts inside the Teams window itself](/tipy/teams-zkratky-schuzky) — combined, the two mean you can always control your microphone entirely from the keyboard: system-wide via Win+Alt+K, in-app via Ctrl+Shift+M.
