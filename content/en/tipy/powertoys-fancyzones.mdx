Windows' built-in half-screen snapping isn't enough once you have a wide monitor or work with more than two windows at a time. PowerToys FancyZones is a free module from Microsoft that lets you design your own custom grid of zones — and windows then snap into it on their own, exactly where they belong.

## A typical scenario

You've got a 34-inch monitor and need email, a data spreadsheet, and notes open side by side while you work. Without FancyZones, you drag windows around by hand, eyeball their size by dragging a corner, and once you've got them arranged correctly, all it takes is closing and reopening one window and the whole layout falls apart — you start over. With FancyZones, you design a grid once, save it, and from then on you just drag a window while holding a key — it snaps into a precisely defined zone on screen, the same way every time.

## How to do it

1. Install **PowerToys** from the Microsoft Store (a free set of tools straight from Microsoft, not an unofficial add-on).
2. In PowerToys, open the **FancyZones** module and click **Launch layout editor** — either from the app window or with the shortcut <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>`</kbd>.
3. In the editor, design a grid suited to your needs — say, three columns side by side, or a combination of one larger main window and two smaller side ones. The editor also offers ready-made layout templates you can just tweak.
4. Save the layout, and from then on drag windows while **holding Shift** — as soon as you release a window over one of the zones, it snaps in and fills its exact dimensions.
5. You can set a different layout for each monitor if you have several, and FancyZones can remember which zone recurring apps belong to.

## The best tools

- **PowerToys FancyZones** — free, from Microsoft, the most complete solution for custom window layouts on Windows.
- **Windows' built-in window snapping** (Win + arrows) — enough on its own, no install needed, for simpler splits into halves or quarters.
- **DisplayFusion** — a paid tool with more advanced multi-monitor management, useful once FancyZones stops being enough for a very complex monitor setup.

## What you get out of it

- **Time:** roughly 10 minutes a day saved on manually moving and rearranging windows, especially when working on a wider monitor.
- **Clarity:** every app has its fixed spot, so you orient yourself faster in a split screen.
- **Consistency:** the layout doesn't disappear after closing and reopening an app — you don't need to rebuild it constantly.
- **Less eye and hand strain:** less manual dragging and eyeballing window sizes with the mouse.

## Pro tip

PowerToys includes other useful modules worth trying too: **PowerRename** for bulk-renaming files by pattern (say, adding a date to dozens of photo filenames at once), and **PowerToys Run**, a fast app and file launcher on the shortcut <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Space</kbd>.
