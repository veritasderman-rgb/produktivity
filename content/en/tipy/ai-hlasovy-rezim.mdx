The best ideas don't show up at the keyboard — they show up on a walk, in the car, or on the way to lunch, when your brain finally has room to think without a screen and notifications in the way. Voice mode in AI apps is built to make use of exactly that time: you talk like you would to a colleague, AI responds, asks questions, and helps you carry an idea to completion, without you having to type a word or even look at the screen.

## A typical scenario

A manager has to present a new strategy to leadership in two days, but the idea so far only exists in her head as a fog she can't untangle at her computer. Without this tip, she sits down, stares at a blank document, and writing goes slowly because the thoughts still aren't sorted — an hour passes and she has barely a few slides. With this tip, on the way to lunch she opens voice mode, talks through the presentation's structure and key arguments out loud with AI, lets it push back on the weak spots — and back at the office, all that's left is opening her notes and writing the final version, because the hardest part — sorting out her thinking — is already done.

It requires no preparation at all — just a phone and the willingness to talk a problem through out loud.

## How to do it

1. In the ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude mobile app, start voice mode, usually via a microphone or headphone icon in the chat.
2. Describe the problem you're working through: “I need to think through the structure of a presentation for leadership, the main topic is…”
3. Talk to AI like a sparring partner — let it ask follow-up questions, respond, change direction when something occurs to you.
4. When you're done, ask for a summary: “Send me a written, bullet-point summary of our conversation.”
5. Once back at your computer, just edit the summary and fold it into the final document.
6. If another idea hits you while you're still walking, feel free to return to the conversation and add to it — voice mode works like an ongoing notebook, not a one-off conversation.

## The best tools

Any of the following can be launched right on your phone, with no setup or extra install needed.

- **ChatGPT voice mode** — built into the mobile app, smooth conversation, and you can even interrupt it.
- **Gemini Live** — Google's equivalent, well integrated with Android phones.
- **Claude voice mode** — a good fit if you already use Claude for longer texts and want to pick up where the voice conversation left off.
- **A voice recorder or dictaphone as a backup** — if you're out of signal, record your monologue and have AI process it later once you're connected.

## What you get out of it

- **Time:** twenty minutes of walking replaces an hour of sitting in front of a blank page.
- **Sanity:** you shape an idea before you even sit down to write — less blank-page pressure and less performance pressure under a deadline.
- **Quality:** thinking out loud often exposes weaknesses in an argument faster than writing does, because you have to respond to follow-up questions on the spot, not just on paper.
- **Reclaimed time:** commutes that would otherwise be dead time turn into productive thinking, without costing you time you'd spend elsewhere.

## Pro tip

Voice mode is also great for rehearsing a difficult conversation — ask AI to play the other side of a negotiation, and try your argument out loud before the real conversation happens. If you mention specific numbers or names from internal materials during the call, keep it general — “client A,” “a project with a budget around X” — a voice conversation goes to the AI provider the same way written text does, and you should treat it with the same caution.
